# Answer Key — Auto-Graded Practice/Mock Exam Questions

These are the answers currently wired into the multiple-choice buttons in
`cyber_security_with_all_worksheets.html` (Units 4, 5 and 6 practice/mock
exam tabs). They were derived from general cybersecurity and NCFE Level 2
curriculum knowledge, **not from an official mark scheme** — please have a
tutor spot-check these, especially the ⚠️ flagged ones, before students rely
on them for exam prep.

If any answer needs to change, find the matching `id="u#q#"` button group in
`cyber_security_with_all_worksheets.html` and move `true`/`false` between
the `checkMCQ(this, true/false, '...')` calls for that question.
