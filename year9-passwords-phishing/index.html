Start · 3 minutes

How long would a hacker need to crack your password?

Criminals don't sit and guess passwords by hand. They use software that tries billions of guesses every second. There are two main ways it works:

Brute-force attack: try every possible combination: a, b, c … aa, ab … until one works. Longer passwords with more types of character take far longer.

Dictionary attack: try a list of common passwords and words first, like password, football or qwerty, plus tricks like adding 123 or swapping a for @. These fall in under a second.

Phishing is different: instead of cracking your password, the criminal tricks you into giving it to them, usually with a fake email or text.

Build a strong passphrase

The UK's National Cyber Security Centre recommends using three random words. They're long, so brute force takes ages, and they're easier to remember than xT9#q!2L.
